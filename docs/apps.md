# The Mac and Windows apps

Bamah runs on a server, and you can always use it in a browser. The Mac and Windows apps are the same Bamah in a window of its own, with a menu bar, keys for everything you do in a scene, and the few things a browser tab can't do well. Your stories stay on the server, so the apps, the browser and your phone all show the same scenes.

## The first time

The app asks for your Bamah's address, like `bamah.example.com`. Type it and choose **Open**, then sign in as you would in a browser. The app keeps you signed in. To use another Bamah later, choose **Server…** (in the **Bamah** menu on a Mac, and in **File** on Windows).

The apps aren't from an app store, so the first time you open one, your computer warns you about it:

- **On a Mac:** open Bamah once, and when the Mac says it can't check it, go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to Bamah.
- **On Windows:** when "Windows protected your PC" comes up, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The installer puts Bamah in your Start menu, just for you, and doesn't need an administrator.

## The keys

| What it does | Mac | Windows |
|---|---|---|
| Send what you've written, or let the scene decide who speaks | ⌘↩ | Ctrl+Enter |
| Go on | ⌘] | Ctrl+] |
| Take the last line again | ⌘R | Ctrl+R |
| Write my line | ⌘L | Ctrl+L |
| A "What if" scene | ⇧⌘W | Ctrl+Shift+W |
| Feelings, Memory, Journal | ⌘1, ⌘2, ⌘3 | Ctrl+1, Ctrl+2, Ctrl+3 |
| Bigger text, smaller, actual size | ⌘+, ⌘−, ⌘0 | Ctrl+Plus, Ctrl+Minus, Ctrl+0 |
| A new story, a new scene | ⌘N, ⇧⌘N | Ctrl+N, Ctrl+Shift+N |
| Settings | ⌘, | Ctrl+, |

Every one of them is in the menus too.

## What else the apps do

- **Drop a file on the window** to bring it in. In a scene, you choose where it goes: into the scene, onto a character, or into the story's journal, where it can be split into entries. Dropping a file on a journal page adds it to that journal. This works in a browser too.
- **Export and Backup** ask where to save, with your computer's own Save window.
- **When a reply comes in while you're in another app,** the Mac bounces Bamah's icon in the Dock and shows a notification with who answered, and Windows flashes Bamah on the taskbar.
- **Links to other sites** open in your browser.
- **On a Mac,** the title bar takes your theme's colour, **New Window** (⌥⌘N) opens a second window, so you can keep a character open beside a scene, and the Mac's own spelling and dictation work in the writing box.
- **On Windows,** the menus sit in the title bar, as in Windows 11's own apps, and F11 is full screen.
